# Product: Phase 2.5 Award-Quality Floor Calibration

## Problem

KINETIC can produce technically correct and original pages that still look far below the quality expected from an award-caliber digital experience. The first real human review rejected every IZANAMI candidate; V2 and V3 were only weak relative positives. Before learning the user’s personal taste, KINETIC must prove that it can produce at least one substantially stronger, human-acceptable direction without sacrificing originality, accessibility, performance, or honesty about visual evidence.

## Success metric

From one new calibration reference and no more than two original candidates, at least one original candidate is judged by the user to be **meaningfully stronger than the first IZANAMI batch** and **ACCEPTABLE_FOR_FURTHER_TASTE_LEARNING**. Measurement occurs in the second human review. A technical pass alone does not count, and `REJECT_ALL` remains a valid outcome.

## Announcement — the blog post before the feature

KINETIC now separates reliable implementation from real design quality. Every calibration direction is planned around one authored signature move, compared against visible reference evidence, and reviewed for composition, typography, art direction, depth, motion, interaction, storytelling, cohesion, and surface fit. The review experience shows desktop, mobile, and important interaction states without pre-selecting a winner. Reviewers can accept a candidate, partially accept useful qualities, or reject the entire batch. Personal taste learning stays paused until the baseline quality floor is genuinely cleared.

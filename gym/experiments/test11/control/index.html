KYOTO · SINCE ALWAYS

A bowl of tea is a door left open.

KŌAN is a tea-ceremony studio where the oldest gestures are practiced slowly, so that the present moment has somewhere to sit.

We do not perform ceremony. We make room for it.

Every gathering begins with silence, water, and attention. The room is prepared before the guest arrives; the guest is prepared before the room is entered. What remains is the simplest exchange two people can share.

Three experiences.

01

The First Bowl

An introduction for those who have never knelt on tatami. One hour, one bowl, no prior knowledge required.

02

The Long Sit

A full seasonal gathering. Three hours, several courses, and the unhurried rhythm of a complete ceremony.

03

The Quiet Study

For returning guests who wish to learn the gestures themselves, across a series of private sessions.

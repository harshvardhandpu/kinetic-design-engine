FROM THE WORKBENCH

We write down what we make, so we know why we made it.

This is not a portfolio. It is a running record of decisions — what we chose, what we discarded, and what the work taught us on the way.

PRACTICE / 01

Slow starts

Every project begins with a week of not designing. We read, we ask, we sit with the problem until it stops being a brief and starts being a question.

PRACTICE / 02

Loud drafts

Our first drafts are deliberately too much. We overshoot, then remove — because you cannot edit a blank page, and restraint is a second pass, not a first instinct.
